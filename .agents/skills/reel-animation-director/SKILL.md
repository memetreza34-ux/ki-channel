# Reel Animation Director

Use this skill for every new or revised AI reel.

For each scene write exactly:

```text
spoken meaning phrase
→ one large primary object
→ one dominant motion
→ one stable result
```

Verify that the primary visual occupies roughly 60 to 78 percent of the animation area, the scene is understandable within two seconds, one to three meaning beats are used, no more than two strong motions compete, the real transcript trigger starts the motion and the result holds at least one second.

Reject cards that merely slide, repeated frame or stack movements, small washed-out visuals, mini-dashboards, topic-only decoration and important statements without a visible demonstration.
