# Animation Plan — Antigravity Context Overload

## Production principle

Use the existing content-aware prototypes exactly as semantic mechanisms. The visible labels, objects, and qualitative state changes must come from the spoken text through the canonical runtime-grounding chain. Do not copy demo `labels` or `values` from `content-render-fixtures.json`.

## Scene mapping

| Scene | Animation ID | Dominant motion | Required visible result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `context-window-context-window-train-v1` | information enters a bounded context lane and older information is displaced | bounded context is visibly crowded without an invented numeric capacity |
| 2 | `relationship-network-dependency-bridge-builder-v1` | strong links grow while weak links recede | relevant vs. weak relationships are visually distinguishable without fake percentages |
| 3 | `retrieval-search-knowledge-magnet-v1` | relevant sources move toward the active context, irrelevant sources remain outside | selective retrieval is obvious without claiming an unspoken source count |
| 4 | `input-output-funnel-compression-output-v1` | inputs converge, irrelevant information exits, relevant information compresses | a compact information core remains without fake discard counters |
| 5 | `generation-answer-loom-v1` | grounded answer text appears step by step from the compact context | final takeaway is readable and no artificial token/probability count is shown |

## Choreography rules

- One dominant explanatory motion per spoken sentence.
- Maximum three strong simultaneous motions.
- Every scene must have readable start, change, and result states.
- Hold the final result long enough to read at phone size.
- Keep subtitles separate from main mechanism labels.
- Preserve the existing prototype look; do not replace them with generic cards.
- No decorative particles unless they carry semantic state.
- No fake progress percentages, counts, capacities, weights, or confidence values.
- No repeated full animation in this reel.

## Visual continuity

Use the same dark premium AI-channel environment across all five scenes. Carry one small neutral "context" visual token between scenes when practical, but do not force a transition if it weakens the explanation. Hard cuts are acceptable and preferred over decorative transitions.
